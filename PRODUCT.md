# FRAGILE Field Atlas

A separate interactive companion to the original FRAGILE animal sculptures. Visitors discover endangered species, their general habitats and contextual conservation facts through a sculptural interaction.

The current requested experience begins with one animal alone, centered on black. A click disassembles it into many triangles, which form the Earth; additional incoming fragments assemble infographic symbols. Drag inspection, the existing animal identities and movements, and species order remain intact.

Audience: visitors to a visual wildlife exhibition. Success: the relationship between animal and habitat is understood through the transformation, and population figures remain correctly scoped and sourced.

Constraints: black background, white IBM Plex Mono text, real white triangular wireframe, no filled faces, no audio. Ten existing species only. Keep the original `fragile/` site separate and untouched; connect the experiences later. Local preview for visual approval; no deployment in this phase.

Authoritative content: `src/data.js`, `src/species-base.js`, `SOURCES.md`, and the individually linked research references. General-region markers are schematic; models are artistic and not to a shared scale.
